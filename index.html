Who we are

Special Olympics Nepal (SON) is a national non-profit recognized by Special Olympics International and the National Sports Council of Nepal. Since 1987 we have built a platform where people with intellectual disabilities are celebrated, not hidden.

Core values

Sport is a human right and a path to dignity, health, and belonging. Through our athletes, every step onto the field challenges stigma.

Education

Inclusive PE and school teams teach empathy and teamwork.

Health

Regular activity reduces health risks and improves mental wellbeing.

Social change

Public events and visibility turn sympathy into respect.

Economic inclusion

Skills and confidence open pathways to training and work.
