Our Programs

Healthy Athletes®

Supporting health beyond the playing field.

Healthy Athletes® provides free health screenings, education and referral opportunities for people with intellectual disabilities. We help our athletes stay healthy so they can train, compete and live fuller lives.

LEARN MORE ABOUT OUR WORK arrow_forward

About The Program

About Healthy Athletes®

Many people with intellectual disabilities face greater health challenges and barriers to care. Healthy Athletes® helps change that.

Through health screenings, education and follow-up referrals, we aim to improve the well-being of our athletes so they can train, compete and live healthier lives. We offer services in multiple disciplines, ensuring holistic care tailored to their unique needs.

Our program also trains healthcare professionals and students to better treat people with intellectual disabilities, creating a more inclusive healthcare system across Nepal.
