Nepal Athletes Shine at Special Olympics Asia Pacific Games

calendar_today May 15, 2026
person Special Olympics Nepal
schedule 5 min read

The Special Olympics Asia Pacific Games in Singapore saw an unprecedented performance from the Nepal delegation, bringing home a record number of medals and inspiring a nation.

In a stunning display of skill, determination, and sportsmanship, the athletes of Special Olympics Nepal have made their mark on the international stage. Competing against thousands of athletes from across the region, our team demonstrated that with the right support and opportunity, there are no limits to what they can achieve.

Triumph on the Track

The athletics events proved to be a particularly strong showing for Nepal. The track and field squad secured multiple podium finishes, highlighted by gold medal performances in both the 100m sprint and the 4x100m relay. The dedication these athletes showed during their grueling training camps in Kathmandu clearly paid dividends.

Beyond the medals, it was the spirit of the Nepal team that captured the hearts of spectators. Their infectious enthusiasm, unwavering support for competitors from other nations, and joyful celebrations embodied the true essence of the Special Olympics movement.

Unified Sports Initiative Takes Center Stage

This year's games also highlighted the growing success of our Unified Sports programs. By pairing athletes with and without intellectual disabilities on the same team, these events foster genuine inclusion and mutual respect. The Nepal Unified Basketball team's silver medal run was a testament to the power of playing together.

Looking Forward

As the team returns home to heroes' welcomes, the focus now shifts to sustaining this momentum. Special Olympics Nepal is committed to expanding its reach, ensuring that more individuals with intellectual disabilities across all provinces have access to sports training and health programs.
